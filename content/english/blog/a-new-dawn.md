A new dawn arrives like a hush in the air,<br>
A warmth so steady, a soul laid bare.

It looks like you, right where we start,<br>
The future unfolding, heart to heart.

The years before carved grace and truth,<br>
A gentle strength, a timeless youth.

The years ahead, I pray they see,<br>
A life where us is destiny.

Your smile is home, your touch feels right,<br>
A promise I trust in every light.

And if life’s road is long and true,<br>
I’m grateful forever begins with you.
